This pass is explicitly authorized as an opt-in goal loop. It does not promote
the ballistic candidate, change policy 3, publish root navigation, commit, push,

## Question and fixed scope

The frozen V14 exit-consistency diagnostic landed 706 of the original 1000
worlds. A mathematical screen suggests that some early destination proposals
are rejected against an instantaneous desired arc even though their finite
powered acquisition and subsequent coast may be clear. That screen is not
executed-flight proof.

Record the actual native query states and independently check the existing
correction through its absolute cutoff, with the ordinary plant, rotation,
paired throttle, body reserve and short-command guard. Check the realized
cutoff's unpowered destination coast and existing landing-angle/braking-room
conditions. Do not invent a terminal suffix or replace live velocity with the
desired velocity.

The probe inherits exit-consistency without changing commands. The bounded
candidate may admit such an early proposal and apply the same finite-acquisition
check while retaining its destination correction. Optional queries run on the
existing 24-tick cadence; declining one must leave the active waypoint and
correction intact. Existing actual safe-coast acceptance can end a correction.
Initial nominal generation, waypoint placement/ranking, recovery choices,
physical guards, deadlines, fuel accounting and terminal control stay unchanged.
The existing negative horizontal waypoint braking-room trigger stays unchanged;
adding a vertical trigger is deferred.
