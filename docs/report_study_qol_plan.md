## Product and evidence contract

Each study declares its question, motivation, changed/fixed factors, stable
variants, explicit contrasts, cohort roles, metric units/eligibility and frozen
claims. The shared presentation consumes a separate versioned neutral projection;
it does not relax persisted campaign or evaluation contracts. Pairing binds cohort,
physical input and scheduled occurrence. Captions never identify an approach.
Unscheduled, missing, unsupported and unverified results remain distinct.

The fixed qualification result never changes with chart selection. Exploratory
selection reports its own count and eligible-pair denominator. Fuel comparisons
require both verified landings. Incoming contact and post-contact endpoint values
remain separate. Statistical intervals come from the audited source analysis;
the renderer does not invent another test or infer acceptance after filtering.

The default timing contrast is 63 to 132 pairs on the 300 fresh originals. The
60 boundaries, 21 one-variant handoff regressions, exposed development and pilot
repeats remain separate and accessible. Latency uses 12 exposed originals and
84 measured selections per profile, never a fictitious 300-point paired dataset.
The default RL contrast is heuristic to frozen RL on 600 original arcs. Its
saved outcome matrix is 443 preserved, 146 gained, three lost and eight shared
misses. Lost originals 355/436/514 are mandatory navigation acceptance cases.

The study overview combines a short authored explanation with fixed claims,
paired outcome transitions, per-original metric differences and separately
labelled performance measurements. Clicking a visual selects the same mission
tree. Cohort, contrast, metric and selection survive detail/back/next navigation.
Existing shared-scale A/B previews and rich details are reused; a synchronized
two-flight viewer is deferred. A timing explanation panel uses recorded candidate
forecasts and selected burn timing without claiming isolated causal attribution.

## Rich visual repairs

Scalar paths must retain visible nonzero geometry at dense sample rates, while
bounding trace overhead. Full samples remain available to hover and evidence.
Throttle commands use a fixed 0–1 domain; paired speed views share their domain.
Vector glyphs show commanded throttle and actual attitude, use bounded pixel
length and screen-space spacing, and remain readable on zoom and mobile.
Rendering, resize, constant/empty data, finite stops and static fallback are
checked independently of control/button presence.
